# VanBanAI

VanBanAI is a graduation-project concept for AI-assisted drafting of Vietnamese business documents, with a human reviewer in control of the final result.

## Project goal

Explore a workflow that collects and confirms user-provided facts, retrieves relevant source material with provenance, drafts document clauses, checks them for unsupported or duplicated content, and exports a reviewed version to Word or PDF.

The initial workflows are an internal equipment handover record and a software services agreement.

## Accuracy and safety

- Ask for missing or conflicting facts instead of guessing.
- Treat generated content as a draft for human review, not legal advice or legal approval.
- Do not mark a legal source as verified or publishable based only on an AI verdict.
- Use synthetic people, organizations, and documents in examples and tests.
